Why Choose Us

Why Choose CHERRS BIM?

Our BIM-driven workflow enables project teams to identify and resolve design conflicts before construction begins, minimizing costly rework and ensuring seamless coordination among all project stakeholders. Our solutions help clients achieve:

  • ✓Highly accurate and coordinated 3D BIM models
  • ✓Early clash detection and resolution
  • ✓Improved interdisciplinary coordination
  • ✓Reduced material waste and construction rework
  • ✓Faster production of construction documentation
  • ✓Enhanced design visualization and constructability reviews
  • ✓Optimized construction planning and scheduling
  • ✓Lower project costs and reduced schedule delays
  • ✓Increased onsite productivity and installation efficiency
  • ✓Reliable as-built documentation for facility management

About Us

A multi-disciplinary team, coordinated by design

Established in 2023, CHERRS BIM is a multinational BIM and engineering consultancy serving the Architecture, Engineering and Construction industry. Our engineers, architects, BIM modelers and technicians build intelligent, data-rich 3D models across mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire protection systems.

We work in Autodesk Revit, Navisworks Manage, AutoCAD, Autodesk Construction Cloud, BIM 360 and Bluebeam Revu — developing every model to the Level of Development each project stage requires, from LOD 100 concept massing through LOD 500 as-built facility management data.
